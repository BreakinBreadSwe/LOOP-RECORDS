# Loop Records

Catalog app for Loop Records, the Stockholm techno and experimental house label (1993 to 1998), and its sublabel Plumphouse Records. Owner: Gordon Cyrus (luvlab.io).

## Working with Gordon

Gordon is blind and uses a screen reader, mostly on an Android phone. Everything built here must work fully with a screen reader: real headings, labelled controls, live-region announcements for status changes, focus management on navigation, no information conveyed by colour or position alone. Keep replies to him in plain, warm prose. No em dashes, no heavy bullet formatting.

## Rules that stand

* Do not mention Breakin' Bread anywhere on the public site. The back office (staff only) may: Gordon asked for a push to breakinbread.com there, so it has a "Breakin Bread" section with "Send everything to Breakin Bread" and a dry run, plus a Breakin Bread order channel. The push (`/api/breakin-bread/push`) needs `BREAKINBREAD_IMPORT_URL` and `BREAKINBREAD_IMPORT_SECRET`; until they are set it says Breakin Bread isn't connected yet. The receiving end is bb-native PR #1 (`/api/admin/label-import`).
* Respect every credit: every person on every release and track is searchable and links to their own page.
* Pasted artwork keeps its own shape. Never crop a sticker or insert in display; use `object-fit: contain`.
* Loop's colours, sampled from the 1993 flyer 7-inch: orange `#FF4400`, royal blue `#1F16B2`, cream. Orange is the accent, blue marks focus.
* Mobile first. Gordon tests on his phone.

## What is in this repository

* `src/app.template.html` is the shared page (public catalog plus back office shell). `src/admin.js` is the back office, spliced in at `/*__ADMIN__*/`. `src/runtime.js` connects the page to Supabase (staff sign-in by email link, live document store, artwork uploads, downloads) by implementing the small `window.claude` API the page was written against.
* `public/brand/` holds the real Loop logo, vectorized from the sleeves with potrace: `loop-spiral.svg` (from the LOOP 012 label) and `loop-wordmark.svg` (the italic LOOP RECORDS lettering from the LOOP 001 sleeve), both `fill="currentColor"`, plus PNGs in black, white and orange at 1600px and `loop-favicon.svg`. The header shows them through CSS masks (spiral in Loop orange, lettering in the text colour); the build swaps `__LOGO_SPIRAL__`/`__LOGO_WORD__` for file paths, or for data URIs in the Claude build.
* Look and feel (Back office, Look and feel; stored in `app_docs` collection `site`, document `look`, readable by everyone via migration 0006): optional light and dark background colours (with a contrast check), background tint from the current release, spiral and lettering colour ("changes with the releases' colours", a fixed colour, or Loop orange / text colour), screensaver on/off and delay (default on, 9 seconds), and custom CSS. Release colours come from `data/cover-colors.json` (strongest colour of each main cover, `scripts/cover_colors.py`), merged as `r.color`; the logo and the tab icon take the release's colour on its page and another catalog colour on each other page change; lettering falls back to the text colour below 3:1 contrast.
* Screensaver: a full-screen mosaic of the sleeves (whole, not cropped) that fades sleeves in and out; `aria-hidden`, never starts during audio, video, typing or an open dialog, and any touch, key, scroll or focus change ends it; the touch that wakes it never activates what is underneath.
* Header: logo with STOCKHOLM 1993–1998 as a line under the lettering at the same width (an SVG with `textLength`), then monochrome icon buttons with `aria-label` and `title` (Releases, People, About, Back office, My page, theme, sign in/out). Theme is one button cycling Match device, Light, Dark. On phones (640px and narrower) the logo gets its own row at full width (up to 420px) with the era line under it, and search plus the icons share the row below; the header stays at the top, slides away while scrolling down and returns on scrolling up, at the top, on focus or on page change. From 900px search sits between logo and icons.
* Home: one row with the label chips (All, Loop, Plumphouse, Licensed) on the left and, right-aligned, ordering (Cat. no., A–Z by artist, Year by release date, Reverse), the count and the Mosaic icon; it wraps to two rows only on phones. Ordering is remembered (`loop-sort`) and also applies to search results and the People page (A–Z or first year). Searches add a Show row: Everything, Releases, People.
* Videos (`#videos`, header icon; `#video-<YouTube id>` links to one): "Loop TV". A stage with the player (YouTube IFrame API, plain embed if it can't load) and the release's sleeve, title and link beside it, tinted with the release colour; the logo takes that colour while it plays. "Play all, one after another" auto-advances; Previous and Next. Below, every unique video (237) as a thumbnail with its sleeve in the corner, grouped by release (catalog order) or by year, flat for A to Z, with the label filters and ordering.
* Discogs profile and Search YouTube on person pages are staff only (`.staffonly`, shown by `revealStaff()` in admin.js).
* `npm run build` (`scripts/build.mjs`) writes `public/index.html`, `public/runtime.js`, `public/catalog.json`. Never edit those by hand. `public/art/` holds the pasted cover images.
* `data/catalog.json` is the full catalog: 62 releases, 156 people, tracklists, credits, video links. Shape `loop-records.catalog/v0`.
* `api/config.js` hands the browser the public Supabase URL and anon key. `api/breakin-bread/push.js` is the switched-off partner sync. `lib/bundle.js` builds the sync payload; `lib/server.js` holds server helpers.
* `supabase/migrations/0001_loop_backoffice.sql` is the relational schema (labels, people, releases, tracks, credits, splits, products, customers, orders, events, sync deals, staff, with row level security; money in öre). `0003_audio.sql` adds the public `audio` bucket for track sound files (staff upload, everyone plays, 500 MB per file allowed by the bucket; the Supabase plan's own upload limit also applies). `0002_app_docs.sql` adds the document store the back office uses today (`app_docs`), the public `artwork` storage bucket and sync-log columns. `supabase/seed.sql` loads the whole catalog, idempotent.
* Track sound files: staff see "Add sound file for <position> <title>" under each track on a release page; visitors get a native audio player playing the full track (Gordon chose full tracks, not previews). One `app_docs` document per track in collection `audio`, id `<release id>_<position>`. The partner bundle carries them as `audioUrl` on tracks and works.
* `tests/` runs with `npm test` (node --test). Run `npm test && npm run build` before committing.
* `data/catalog.json` is built from `data/discogs/` (raw Discogs release data) and `data/youtube.json` (extra videos found by search) by `scripts/build_catalog.py`. It carries no images; `data/artwork.json` maps release ids to cover images in `public/art/` (233 images, all 62 releases): `<id>-<n>.jpg` are the 59 images Gordon pasted for 18 releases in the Claude-hosted version (copied from its store on 7 Nov 2026; these always win), `<id>-discogs-<n>.jpg` are every Discogs image (front, back, labels, inserts) for the other 44 (the six "related" releases included, via `--include-related`), fetched by `scripts/fetch_discogs_art.py` at Discogs's limit of 25 a minute, and `lib/catalog.js` merges the two for the website and the partner sync. `data/artwork-db/` and `data/artwork-manifest.csv` record what was pasted in the Claude-hosted version.
* Python build scripts (Python 3, no dependencies): `build_catalog.py` rebuilds `data/catalog.json`, `make_seed.py` regenerates `supabase/seed.sql`, `build_claude_app.py` rebuilds the Claude-hosted version into `dist/` (raw catalog, no pasted covers, since that version keeps its own artwork store), `fetch_discogs_art.py` fetches missing Loop and Plumphouse covers from Discogs one at a time and lists them in `data/artwork.json`. The old `build_site.py` is replaced by `npm run build`.

## Where things stand

The Claude-hosted version is live as a private artifact on claude.ai; that is where Gordon pastes covers today and its data lives in that artifact's own store. This repository runs the same page as a normal website on Vercel with Supabase behind it. Deploy needs `SUPABASE_URL`, `SUPABASE_ANON_KEY`, `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY`, `PUBLIC_SITE_URL` (see `.env.example`). Supabase project `lvpayzovumfpjkbaiwhy` ("LOOP RECORDS", own organization, free plan) has migrations 0001 to 0006 and the seed applied; the site is https://loop-records-mocha.vercel.app and Supabase's sign-in links point there. Access levels (migration 0005, `public.profiles`): everyone who signs up is a customer; admins set customer, artist or admin in Back office, People and access. Artists are linked to a person (`person_key`) and may add covers and sound files only on releases where that person is a release artist or credited (`can_edit_release`, enforced in the database and storage rules); they get a My page link. Admin role keeps `public.staff` in sync. An email in `public.staff_invites` (migration 0004) becomes admin on first sign-in. Invited: gordoncyrus@gmail.com (staff), joachim@streetstar.se. Vito is not hands-on yet. Sign-in emails still use Supabase's built-in mailer (2 a hour, template can't be changed on free) until custom SMTP is set up.
